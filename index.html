Get your Builder lineups into DraftKings from your phone. Use your phone's web browser for DraftKings here, not the DraftKings app: the app can't take a file, the website can.

Android: skip the file hunt. Install FORGE as an app once. After that, when your entries file downloads, open it from the download message or your Downloads, tap Share, pick FORGE, and it lands here already filled.
In Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  1. Join your contests. In DraftKings, enter every contest you want with any lineup (or reserve your entries). You'll replace those lineups in a minute.
  2. Download your entries file. On the DraftKings website, open Lineups → Edit Entries and download your entries (DKEntries.csv). Open DraftKings Edit Entries The page is draftkings.com/entry/upload. On a phone, if the DraftKings app opens instead, type that address into your browser.
  3. Fill it here.

    Can't see the file? iPhone: tap Choose File, then Browse → Downloads. Android: if photos open, tap Browse or the ☰ menu → Downloads. The file is usually named DKEntries.csv.

  4. Upload it back. On DraftKings (Lineups → Edit Entries) tap Upload and pick the filled file (it's in your Downloads / Files). DraftKings shows how many entries were updated.
Sunday late swap (Classic). After the early games start, download your entries file again from DraftKings (Lineups → Edit Entries), choose it in step 3, then press the button. FORGE keeps every player whose game has started and re-picks the rest from players in games that haven't, using the latest projections and FORGE's Classic rules (QB stacked with his own WR/TE, no QB with his own RB or DST, nobody facing your DST). Only entries that gain at least 0.5 projected points are changed.

Live slates

The edge is constructionFORGE's lineups beat the field by about 11 points per lineup on average, but its sims don't pick the right captain more often than the crowd does.
Cheap players are overratedThe prop market projects $1,500-and-under players at about twice what they score. FORGE corrects for it.
Skip TE, K and DST captainsThey almost never win. Removing them helped every builder we tested.
Cash games want a floorFor double-ups the best-projected lineup of any shape cleared the cash line in 86% of tested fields.

FORGE is a research system, not a picks service. Every rule it uses was written down before it was tested, then checked against real contests.

1 · Market projectionsPlayer props from DraftKings and FanDuel are converted to expected Showdown points, captured automatically every two hours.
2 · Simulation100,000 correlated game simulations estimate each player's range and the chance he's the top scorer.
3 · The hybrid buildHalf the lineups are the best 5-1 stacks; half come from a payout simulator against a modeled field. The mix hedges whether one team dominates.
4 · Graded liveAfter every game, all of FORGE's sets are graded on the real contest. A builder that falls behind gets switched out.
