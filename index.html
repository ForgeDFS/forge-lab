About these numbers

Projections come from the betting markets: DraftKings and FanDuel player props, converted to DraftKings Showdown points. Very cheap players get pulled toward what players at their price actually score. Top scorer is the chance, across 100,000 simulated games, that the player is the game's highest scorer, which makes him the ideal captain.

Where FORGE's own model disagrees with the book

FORGE's chance of going over each posted line from its own stat model (no prop prices used), next to the book's. Research, not picks: on 79 past games FORGE's numbers added information beyond the prices (backtest), but its disagreements did not make money there (+1% to +3%, inside the noise) and Week 6 did not confirm. A paper record starts this week. Known lean: FORGE's model sits on the under for catches and yards in general (it averaged 44-46% overs where 49-50% hit), so an under here is often that lean, not a read on the player. Touchdown props are left out here: FORGE's touchdown numbers swing too much on a few recent games and were clearly worse than the book's in the test.

FORGE is a research system, not a picks service. Every rule it uses was written down before it was tested, then checked against real contests. The Builder starts on exactly what FORGE enters.

1 · Market projectionsPlayer props from the sportsbooks are turned into expected DraftKings points. Lines are saved the moment FORGE first sees them, then checked again about 2 hours, 1 hour and 15 minutes before every lock.
2 · SimulationThousands of correlated game simulations give each player a range of outcomes and a chance to be the game's top scorer.
3 · Captain poolFORGE Pick only captains the 3 players most likely to be the top scorer, with more lineups on the likeliest. The top-scoring player was the winning captain in most of the 18 Showdowns we studied.
4 · The hybrid buildHalf the lineups are the best stacks with 5 players from one team; half come from a payout simulator that plays your lineups against a modeled field using FORGE's ownership forecast.
5 · Graded liveAfter every game, FORGE's lineups are graded on the real contest. A change only goes live after it tests better on past contests.
